How WhatsApp Branding & Company Logos Work in Production

Best practices for ensuring customer trust when receiving OTPs and transactional notifications.

1. Use WhatsApp Business App

Install the free WhatsApp Business app on your phone with a dedicated business SIM or eSIM. Set your Brand Logo as the Profile Picture, official company name, category, website, and support email.

2. How Customers See It

When Verifinder sends messages through this account, the customer’s phone displays your Company Logo, business name, and an official "Business Account" badge, creating high brand trust.

3. Dedicated vs Personal

Do not use your personal WhatsApp number for production OTPs. Keep your personal WhatsApp separate and use a dedicated line for Verifinder so personal messages never mix with automated customer verification.
